# 3. Training Types

The application provides two primary types of training: **Easy Walking** and **HR Zone 2 Activity**.

The training type is determined based on the user's characteristics and health condition.

### 3.1. Easy Walking

Easy Walking is a casual walking exercise performed at light intensity.

This training type is assigned to users who require a gentler exercise approach based on identified conditions, including:

- Users in the obese BMI category.
- Users with specific health conditions who are deemed suitable for this training type based on health screening results.

Easy Walking does not require users to reach or maintain Heart Rate Zone 2.

### 3.2. HR Zone 2 Activity

HR Zone 2 Activity is an aerobic workout focused on maintaining the user's heart rate within Zone 2 during the main exercise phase.

The activity can be performed through walking, jogging, or running according to the user's capabilities.

During the workout, the application provides guidance to help users reach and maintain their heart rate within the target range.

### 3.3. Training Type Assignment

The training type determination is made based on the **Final Assessment** during onboarding, utilizing the user's Health Screening results and BMI category:

- **HR Zone 2 Activity:** Assigned when the user has **no health issues based on screening** and a **BMI < 30**. The user can select a frequency of **3–5 sessions per week** and session durations within the available options.
- **Easy Walking:** Assigned when the user **does not meet one or both criteria**, including identified health conditions from screening and/or **BMI ≥ 30**. The Initial Training Plan is set to **3 sessions per week, 15 minutes each**.

The assigned training type serves as the foundation for weekly plan generation and the execution of each session.
